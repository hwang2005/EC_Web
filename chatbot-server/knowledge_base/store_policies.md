## Chinh sach chat luong
- Cam ket 100% tuoi moi, dung nguon goc nhu mo ta.
- Doi/tra mien phi neu hang dap, hong hoac sai san pham.
- Hoan tien 100% neu khong dat chat luong.
- Tiep nhan khieu nai trong vong 24 gio sau giao hang, khuyen nghi gui anh minh chung de xu ly nhanh.

## Chinh sach don hang
- Trang thai don hang: Cho xu ly -> Dang xu ly -> Dang giao -> Da giao / Da huy.
- Huy don trong vong 5 phut sau khi dat, khi don con o trang thai Cho xu ly hoac Dang xu ly.
- Tai khoan consumer co the mua hang, luu yeu thich, gui danh gia va theo doi don.
- Tai khoan seller co the quan ly san pham, don hang va thong ke.

## Goi dang ky dinh ky
- Ho tro giao hang dinh ky theo tuan, 2 tuan/lan hoac hang thang.
- Co the chon ngay giao, khung gio giao va phuong thuc thanh toan.
- Co the tam dung, tiep tuc hoac huy goi.
